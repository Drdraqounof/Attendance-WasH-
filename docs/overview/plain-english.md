# AttendPoint — plain English

AttendPoint helps managers track when people miss work, show up late, or leave early, without digging through text messages.

## What it does

1. Workers send attendance texts (late, sick, no-show, and so on).
2. The system turns those texts into **points**. More points means more risk.
3. Managers see right away who's okay, who to watch, and who needs a call.

## Who uses it

Everyone signs in with their own account and works in one of two roles:

- **HR** sees every station. HR can change an employee's status and adjust the attendance policy settings.
- **Supervisors** see only the employees at their own station. If a supervisor makes a mistake, it stays inside their station and can be undone.

## Risk levels

Points count over a rolling 12 months:

- **Clear:** 0 points
- **Low:** 1–3 points
- **Elevated:** 4–7 points
- **At risk:** 8–11 points
- **Critical:** 12–15 points
- **Termination threshold:** 16 or more points

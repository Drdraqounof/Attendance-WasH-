# AttendPoint — plain English

AttendPoint helps managers track when people miss work, show up late, or leave early — without digging through text messages.

## What it does

1. Workers send attendance texts (late, sick, no-show, etc.).
2. The system turns those texts into **points** (more points = more risk).
3. Managers see who’s okay, who to watch, and who needs a call — right away.

## Risk levels (demo)

- **Clear** — low points, no worry  
- **Watch** — some points, keep an eye on them  
- **At risk** — high points, act soon  

## Important

This build uses **demo data**. Real SMS intake is not hooked up yet.
